The Fishing Capital of the Pacific

Hawaii sits in the middle of the Pacific Ocean where deep, nutrient-rich waters attract some of the world's most prized game fish. The Kona Coast of the Big Island is legendary—more Pacific blue marlin over 1,000 pounds have been caught here than anywhere else on Earth.

Whether you're a seasoned angler chasing granders or a family wanting to reel in your first mahi-mahi, Hawaii's fishing charters offer something for everyone. Most boats provide all gear, tackle, and even help filleting your catch.

Half-day trips run 4-6 hours, full-day trips 8+ hours. Share charters are affordable; private charters give you the boat to yourself.

Types of Fishing Trips

🚤 Deep Sea Sport Fishing

Chase big game offshore—marlin, tuna, mahi-mahi, wahoo. Trolling in deep blue water miles from shore.

  • Half-day or full-day trips
  • All gear provided
  • Experienced crew assists
  • Keep some of your catch

🏝️ Bottom Fishing

Anchor over reefs and ledges for snapper, grouper, and island fish. More relaxed pace, steady action.

  • Great for families
  • More fish, smaller size
  • Calmer experience
  • Delicious eating fish

🌅 Shore Fishing Tours

Guided shore fishing with local experts who know the best spots. Learn techniques for bonefish, trevally, and more.

  • No boat required
  • Learn local techniques
  • Budget-friendly
  • Flexible scheduling

🤿 Spearfishing

Freedive or scuba for your catch. Guided spearfishing trips teach technique and take you to the best reefs.

  • Swimming required
  • Gear provided
  • Unique experience
  • Cook your catch

Fish You'll Catch

Pacific Blue Marlin

The ultimate trophy. 200-1,000+ lbs

Mahi-Mahi

Beautiful & delicious. 10-50 lbs

Yellowfin Tuna (Ahi)

Prized for sashimi. 20-200 lbs

Wahoo (Ono)

Fast & furious. 20-80 lbs

Striped Marlin

Acrobatic fighter. 80-200 lbs

Shortbill Spearfish

Light tackle fun. 20-60 lbs

Best Fishing by Island

🌋 Big Island (Kona)

World capital of blue marlin fishing. Deep water close to shore creates perfect conditions year-round. Most charter options.

🏝️ Maui

Excellent marlin grounds off Lahaina. The channel between Maui and Lanai holds mahi-mahi and ono.

🌺 Oahu

Charters from Ko Olina and Kewalo Basin. Yellowfin tuna, mahi-mahi, and marlin. Most accessible from Waikiki.

🌿 Kauai

Less crowded waters off the Na Pali Coast. Marlin, tuna, and excellent bottom fishing. Fewer boats = more fish.
