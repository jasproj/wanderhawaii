The LineUp at Wai Kai

Oahu's wave pool. A machine-made wave breaks on a standing barrier in Ewa Beach, 100 feet wide, with 30, 65 and 100-foot channels sized to your skill. Around it sits a 52-acre lagoon with a floating obstacle course, paddle craft and cabanas. It opened in February 2023.

The park

A wave you can book by the 45 minutes

Per the park's own opening announcement, The LineUp at Wai Kai opened February 10, 2023. The wave uses Citywave technology. Surf Park Central names Shane Beschen, a former world number two and X Games gold medalist, as a partner in the operating company; Haseko, the developer behind the Hoakalei resort, is the master developer, and Surf Park Management of San Diego is named as consultant and operations manager.

The wave runs in three channels: 30, 65 and 100 feet. A standard surf session is 45 minutes after a short orientation, with helmet, soft-top board and leash included, per the park. The lagoon side sells day passes: the AquaVenture and AquaPeakz floating obstacle courses, a Paddle Pass that swaps between watercraft, a keiki course for ages 4 to 6, cabanas, boat cruises and a splash pad. The park publishes no general manager or instructor list, so we do not name one.

The one to book

Surf Lesson

A 45-minute first-timer session on the wave with an instructor. Ages 10 and up. The rate line shows an 80-pound minimum and the headline shows 90 pounds, so confirm the weight limit when you book. A VIP lesson adds perks; the whole session can also be bought out for a group of up to 12.

The park says it requires guests to swim and hold their breath for ten seconds, and ages 13 and under need a parent or guardian present.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Wai Kai, 91-1621 Keoneula Boulevard, Ewa Beach.

Hours

The park's pages disagree on hours and on which days activities run; use the booking calendar.

Ages

Surf sessions 10 and up. Lagoon passes 7 and up, with a keiki course for 4 to 6 with an adult.

Cancellation

Per the park's FAQ: 48 hours or more ahead gets a refund less $5; 24 to 48 hours gets a rebook or gift card; inside 24 hours is forfeited. The park may cancel for safety and offers a reschedule or refund.

Shuttle

Shuttle-included passes and standalone round trips from Waikiki and Ko Olina are listed on the booking page.

Questions

Good to know

Where is it?

Ewa Beach, about the leeward end of Oahu, with shuttles sold from Waikiki and Ko Olina.

What does it cost?

Surf lesson $125, 65-foot session $75, lagoon day pass $75, Ultimate $164. Checked October 1, 2026.

Do I need experience?

No for the surf lesson; the 65-foot session is aimed at people who already stand up. Swimming ability is required.

Can kids come?

Surf sessions are 10 and up; lagoon passes start at 4 with an adult for the keiki course.

What if it rains or the wave is down?

The park says it may cancel for safety and offers a reschedule or refund.
