Best Kiteboarding School

Action Sports Maui

A wind-sports school that dates to 1996. It calls itself Hawaii's original kiteboarding school and says it was among the first to bring wingsurfing to the islands. The menu is kiteboarding, kitefoil, wingsurf, windsurf, surf and stand-up paddle, with kids classes for most of them. It is family-owned.

The school

Born in Maui's windsurfing scene

The About page says Action Sports Maui was founded in 1996 out of the island's pro-windsurfing community, calls the operation family-owned and run, and says it is part of Maui Sports Unlimited alongside Surf Club Maui and Big Kahuna Adventures. It does not name its owners, so we do not. The home page says over 29 years and Tripadvisor says over 30; we use the founding year.

Instructors named on the site by first name include Gonzo, Kevin, Fran and Francesco on kiteboarding, Sarge and Jonathan on surfing, with Suzie handling scheduling. Wind sports run on Maui's north shore and surf lessons on the south shore, so the meeting spot depends on the lesson; your confirmation names it.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The operator site gives 1900 South Kihei Road, Kihei; Tripadvisor lists Kahului. Wind sports are on the north shore and surf on the south shore, so your confirmation names the spot.

Included

Gear is included on the lessons that say so, such as the windsurf and multi-day kite courses.

Wind

Wind-dependent lessons can move or cancel; reviewers say rescheduling was handled openly.

Ages

Kiting for kids and the windsurf kids camp start at 5; the kids surf camp is for ages 10 to 15.

Questions

Good to know

How long has it run?

Since 1996, per its About page.

What does a kite lesson cost?

$300 shared, $375 private for 2.5 hours, $450 private for 3 hours. Checked October 1, 2026.

Can I learn in a few days?

Yes: three-day ($1,125) and five-day ($1,825) private courses.

Is there something for kids?

Yes: kids surf classes, a windsurf kids camp and kiting for kids from age 5.

What if there is no wind?

Wind lessons can be rescheduled; confirm the refund terms when you book.
