Polynesian Adventure Tours

The long-running land-tour company on all four main islands. It began in 1977 as a van sightseeing outfit in Waikiki and still sells the classics: Pearl Harbor, the Oahu circle-island loop, Haleakala at sunrise, the Road to Hana, the volcano from Hilo, Waimea Canyon on Kauai. Everything books through one FareHarbor calendar.

The company

A 1977 van-tour company, now part of a coach group

Per the operator and Wikipedia, Polynesian Adventure Tours was founded in 1977 by Bob George and Don Brown. Its site calls it family-owned and locally operated, and press releases show that Transportation Charter Services acquired it in October 2017. The operator's footer lists TCS U.S., TCS Entertainer Coach, TCS Canada and Royal Coach as its family of companies.

The fleet runs from luxury SUVs and vans to a 24-seat mini bus and 56-seat coach. Leaders named in 2019 and 2020 releases include President Terry Fischer, COO Marc Rubenstein and VP Kelly Camps; current titles are unconfirmed. Tripadvisor says Luau Kalamaku on Kauai operates under Polynesian Adventure's management.

The one to book

Road to Hana Adventure Tour

A guided day on the Road to Hana from Maui hotels, about 12 hours with a 6 am start, in air-conditioned transport. A guide narrates and drives the curves so you don't. Children 3 to 11 must book alongside an adult; infants sit on a lap.

The company's FAQ gives a full refund on land tours up to 48 hours before pickup, with 72 hours for the sunrise tour; weather refunds are not guaranteed.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Hotel pickup from designated locations in Waikiki, on Maui, Kauai and in Hilo; call Maui sunrise pickups confirm at

Pearl Harbor

Tours require group transport. Children must be 42 inches for the Ford Island control tower.

Meals

Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free meals on request where included.

Cancellation

Full refund up to 48 hours before pickup on land tours, 72 hours for Haleakala Sunrise, 7 days for one-day and helicopter tours. Weather refunds are not guaranteed.

Ages

Children under 21 wait outside during distillery tastings on Best of Maui.

Questions

Good to know

Is it one company on every island?

Yes: Oahu, Maui, Kauai and Big Island tours sell under one name and one booking calendar.

What does it cost?

Pearl Harbor from $58, Oahu circle $139, Haleakala Sunrise $243, Road to Hana $249, Hilo volcano $213. Checked September 30, 2026.

Who owns it?

Transportation Charter Services, which acquired it in 2017 per press releases. It was founded in 1977.

Is the refund policy flexible?

48 hours for most land tours, 72 for Haleakala Sunrise; weather refunds are not guaranteed.

Is it related to Luau Kalamaku?

Tripadvisor says the luau operates under Polynesian Adventure's management.
