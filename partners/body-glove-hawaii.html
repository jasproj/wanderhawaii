The company

Maggie Brown's boat since 1988

Body Glove Cruises started in Kona in 1988; the boat and the company belong to Kanoa, Inc., and the Body Glove name is used under license. Maggie Brown came to Hawaii in 1971 from a family that had been building and running boats since the 1700s, joined the company at the start as marketing director, and has owned and run it as president since. She has called boating her church and the place she found her peace.

Kanoa II replaced the original 55-foot Kanoa: 65 feet, two decks, a fully shaded booth deck and a bench deck, wheelchair access on the lower level, solar panels, hot showers and a bar. The Kona Explorer, a 41-foot raft with twin 350 Suzukis and a restroom, arrived in August 2022 to run the coast 25 miles in either direction. The company is certified by the Sustainable Tourism Association of Hawaii, Dolphin SMART compliant, runs plastic-free, and has offset 550 tons of carbon since 2019.

The one to book

The snorkel and dolphin cruise

Four and a half hours on Kanoa II. Continental breakfast leaving the pier, spinner dolphins along the coast most mornings, then about two hours anchored at Red Hill with snorkel gear, flotation toys, stand-up paddleboards, the slide and the jump. Barbecue burgers for lunch, a vegan taro burger if you ask, and a bar with local spirits and craft beer.

Lifeguards on duty, instruction for first-timers, and a shaded deck for anyone who would rather watch. All ages.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The Body Glove store in Ilima Court, 75-5629 Kuakini Highway, then a one-block walk to Kailua Pier in front of the King Kamehameha hotel. Board 30 minutes before departure.

Included

Snorkel gear and instruction, flotation, paddleboards, the slide and jump, lifeguards, breakfast and lunch on the morning cruise, dinner and a cocktail on the dinner cruise, reef-safe sunscreen on the raft.

Bring

A towel. Swimsuit under your clothes saves time. No full-face masks.

Raft rules

Kona Explorer takes ages 6 and up; not for pregnant guests or anyone with back or neck problems.

Cancellation

Terms vary by party size; check at booking.

Questions

Good to know

Where does the boat leave from?

Kailua Pier in downtown Kona. Check in at the store on Kuakini Highway a block away.

Where do you snorkel?

The catamaran goes to Red Hill. The raft picks from Pawai Bay, Puu Ohau, Kealakekua, Honaunau and Alahaka Bay by conditions.

Is there a whale guarantee?

Yes, December through March: no sighting and you sail again free.

What does it cost?

Snorkel cruise $169.99, dinner cruise $174.99, whale watch $139.99, raft snorkel $130, raft sunset $120. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can small children come?

Yes on Kanoa II, at $49.99 for 5 and under. The raft is 6 and up.
