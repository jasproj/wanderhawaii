The company

The first new catamaran on Waikiki Beach in twenty years

Only a handful of catamaran licenses exist for Waikiki Beach, and Holokai was the first new boat to get one in more than two decades. She was designed by Morrelli & Melvin and built by Schooner Creek Boat Works in Portland, Oregon, then sailed to Hawaii by way of San Francisco in the summer of 2015; the crew fished for dinner when the trade winds quit on them.

The company is women-owned and run by president Soo Kim, who has said her greatest reward is seeing her staff thrive. The captains are Coast Guard licensed, the snorkel guides hold CPR and first-aid cards, and part of the proceeds go to the Hawaii Wildlife Fund. Reviews name the crew constantly: captains Mitchell, Kyle, Ryan and Jamie, guides Abby, Alec, Xavier, Jalen and Aslin.

The one to book

Turtle Canyon Snorkel

Two and a half hours, split about evenly between sailing and snorkeling, at 9 am or noon daily. Turtle Canyon is a shallow reef off Waikiki where green sea turtles come to have their shells cleaned by reef fish, which is why the sightings are so consistent. Mask, fins, snorkel, vest and an in-water guide are included, along with snacks, water, juice, soda, reef-safe sunscreen and motion-sickness tablets.

Beer and seltzers are $5 cash on this one; the open bar is for the afternoon and sunset sails. All ages; anyone under 18 needs a parent aboard and a signed waiver.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The Holokai desk inside the Outrigger Reef Waikiki Beach Resort, 2169 Kalia Road, past the front desk on the right, across from Monkeypod Kitchen. Check in 30 minutes before; the crew walks you to the beach 15 minutes before departure. Late arrivals are not refunded.

Parking

Valet at the Embassy Suites, 201 Beach Walk, $15 for four hours with validation.

Boarding

From the sand, barefoot, up the bow stairs. Wear shoes that come off easily; expect to get your feet wet. Not wheelchair accessible.

Bring

Towel for the snorkel, a long sleeve for the evening sails, and ID if you are drinking. No outside alcohol.

Cancellation

Full refund or rebook with 24 hours' notice for parties of eight or fewer; 48 hours for nine or more.

Questions

Good to know

Where does it board?

From the beach in front of the Outrigger Reef in Waikiki. Check in at the desk inside the hotel, then walk down to the sand.

Will we see turtles?

Turtle Canyon is a reef cleaning station, so sightings are consistent. Reviews routinely count ten or more.

Is the bar included?

Drinks are included on the afternoon, sunset, fireworks and whale sails. On the snorkel, beer and seltzers are $5 cash.

What does it cost?

Turtle snorkel $80, afternoon sail $50, sunset $80, fireworks $85, whale watch $50. Children 0 to 3 free. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can kids come?

Yes, all ages on every sail except Friday sunsets, which are 21 and over.
