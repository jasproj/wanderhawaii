The company

Owned by a nonprofit since 1980

Pacific Whale Foundation was founded on Maui in 1980 by Greg Kaufman, who died in 2018. It is a 501(c)(3), and it fully owns PacWhale Eco-Adventures along with the Ma'alaea Ocean Store and the online shop; by the foundation's account, the profits fund its research, education and conservation work. The company says it has carried more than six million guests. It is Dolphin SMART certified, and its crews include marine-biology graduates and Red Cross-certified staff.

The fleet is five boats: Ocean Spirit, a 65-foot sailing catamaran with a slide and a full bar; Ocean Explorer, a 54-foot cat; Ocean Voyager and Ocean Guardian, 65-foot power cats that carry 90 on snorkel trips; and Kai Kō, a sloop-rigged sailing cat added in 2025. The August 2023 fire destroyed one boat and damaged two. PacWhale returned to Lahaina Harbor on June 1, 2026, after operating from Ma'alaea.

The one to book

Molokini and Turtle Arches snorkel

Five hours from Ma'alaea. Breakfast of banana bread, croissants, pineapple and POG on the way out, the crater and the turtle reef for the morning, and a grilled lunch on the way home. Unlimited soda, juice and water, plus one premium drink for guests 21 and over with ID. Masks, fins, snorkels, flotation and a snorkel class are included; optical masks are available and towels are not.

Naturalists narrate the ride. The company says if you can't swim, don't snorkel, and asks anyone with heart, breathing or mobility concerns to check with a doctor first.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Ma'alaea: the Pacific Whale Foundation Ocean Store at 300 Ma'alaea Road, next to Maui Ocean Center, 45 minutes before departure. Lahaina: the check-in point is in your confirmation email. Parking at both harbors is paid.

Included

On the snorkel cruises: breakfast, lunch, soft drinks and a premium drink for 21 and over, all snorkel gear and a class. On whale watches: naturalist commentary, hydrophones and filtered water; the Deluxe sail adds lunch and two premium drinks.

Bring

Sunglasses, hat, a light jacket, a towel, reef-safe sunscreen, and a card; the boats are cashless, with cash accepted for tips only. No outside alcohol.

Access

Lightweight folding wheelchairs only, with a companion. Ocean Voyager and Ocean Guardian have boarding ramps.

Cancellation

48 hours for a refund through Customer Care at 808-249-8811. Whale sightings are guaranteed or you go again free, valid for a year.

Questions

Good to know

Who owns PacWhale?

Pacific Whale Foundation, a Maui nonprofit founded in 1980. The foundation says profits fund its research, education and conservation programs.

Is Lahaina Harbor running again?

PacWhale announced its return to Lahaina Harbor on June 1, 2026, and Lahaina trips show open dates. Your confirmation email names the boat and harbor.

Is there a whale guarantee?

Yes. Whale sightings are guaranteed or you go again free, valid for one year. Whale season is November to April.

What does it cost?

Molokini snorkel $219.95, Lanai and Honolua sails $199.95, whale watch sail $119.95, sunset dinner $159.95 at list price. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can young children come?

Yes on most boats; ages 4 and under ride free. Ocean Explorer takes no children under 7, and the small-group whale watch is 7 and up.
