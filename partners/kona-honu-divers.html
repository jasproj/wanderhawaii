The team

A PADI Five Star center with a published crew

The team page names Jeff Jones as general manager, Sarah Milisen as director of education, Kevin S. as instructor and head of training, and Jeff Milisen as blackwater master and photographer; the owner is listed only as “Marty.” A December 2023 Big Island Now feature names Byron Kay as owner since 2015, while the team page lists Byron as director of marketing, so we leave ownership to the operator to confirm.

The boats are Honu One, a 46-foot Newton with a Coast Guard limit of 49 that the company caps at 18 divers on the manta dive, and Honu Lele, a 33-foot World Cat for six divers and a guide. The company says mantas show about 80 percent of the time; if not, the second dive becomes a night reef dive. Across 1,830 reviews, 2 percent are Terrible; the recurring gripes are a six-to-one diver ratio and an unremarkable first afternoon site.

The one to book

2-Tank Night Manta Dive

Five hours from Honokohau Harbor: a sunset reef dive, then the manta site after dark with dive lights. Guide, tanks (air or free nitrox), weights, dive light, snacks, a hot shower and a marine head are included, per the tour page.

Open Water or higher, a dive within the last two years, no heart conditions; ages 12 to 17 with a parent or guardian, and 10 to 11 only with a private guide. Cancel or rebook up to 72 hours before.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where

The company's slip at Honokohau Harbor, Kailua-Kona; the retail shop is on Luhia Street.

Requirements

Manta dive: Open Water or higher, a dive within two years, no heart conditions, conversational English. Blackwater: 50+ dives, 18+, a dive within six months.

Cancellation

Manta dive: cancel or rebook up to 72 hours ahead. Weather policy not published.

Nitrox

Free on the manta dive, per the tour page.
