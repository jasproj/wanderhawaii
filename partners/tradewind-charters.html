Tradewind Charters

A Waikiki charter company built around the private boat. Tradewind's fleet is three yachts: a 43-foot sailing yacht for six, a 40-foot sailing catamaran for twelve and a 50-foot motor catamaran that takes up to forty-nine. The one per-person trip is the Waikiki group snorkel; everything else is the whole boat. Captain Ken Middleton is president.

The company

A captain's company

BBB lists Captain Ken Middleton as president of Tradewind Charters, and the operator's homepage describes him as founder and owner with more than twenty-five years on the water; the sources disagree on a founding year, so we leave it out. Other captains named on the homepage are Nate and Paul. Crew Gehrig and Marcello are praised in reviews. The homepage says Ken sailed from the West Coast to Hawaii and started the company.

The boats on the booking page: the 43-foot Sunshine sailing yacht (1 to 6 guests), the 40-foot Sea Scape sailing catamaran (1 to 12) and the 50-foot Aloha Kai power catamaran (1 to 49). The operator lists snorkel gear, restrooms, a bar and refreshments. It also sells sea spa cruises with an onboard massage, a Friday fireworks cruise and sailing lessons from $595.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Kewalo Basin, Honolulu. Sources give the address as 1125 or 1009 Ala Moana Boulevard; your confirmation names the slip.

Included

Snorkel gear, restrooms, a bar and refreshments, per the operator; champagne on the sunrise cruise.

Weather

Ask the operator about its written policy; none was published where we looked. A reseller listing states free cancellation up to 3 days ahead.

Kids

The operator lists the boats as family-friendly; the booking page shows no age minimum.
