The company

A mother-and-son company from 2006

Jay Geng and his mother Aling started Wasabi Tours in Kona in 2006. Hawaii Business named it a Small Business Editor's Choice in 2023, when it had passed four thousand five-star reviews and doubled revenue in a year; Geng told the magazine the reputation came from taking care of the staff. Brian Nichols runs operations, Geng handles marketing, and the guides work in English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

The fleet is twelve Mercedes Sprinters with bucket seats, tall ceilings, USB ports and air conditioning, capped at thirteen guests each, plus a GLS SUV for private groups of five. Lunch comes from a commercial kitchen, admission fees are built in, and rain gear rides along. The company is a member of Sustainable Tourism Hawaii and says it follows the Hawaii Ecotourism Association's tenets.

The one to book

Twilight Volcano and Stargazing

Twelve hours, picked up between 9:30 and 11. A coffee farm, the black sand at Punaluu, then Hawaii Volcanoes National Park in the late afternoon: the Nahuku lava tube, Chain of Craters Road, and the crater lookouts as the light goes, which is when any glow shows. Dinner at a restaurant near the park, then stargazing on the way home near Mauna Kea.

Lava is never guaranteed; the company says conditions change by the hour and this tour is the best shot. Lunch, dinner, park fees and jackets are included. Ages 5 and up.

Before you go

Know before you go

Pickup

Major hotels in Keauhou, Kona, Waikoloa and Kohala. Hilo hotels, Hilo airport and the Hilo cruise port are for private tours; Kona airport on request.

Included

Lunch from the company kitchen (teriyaki chicken, panko fish, purple potato, rice; dietary needs with notice), all admission fees, jackets and rain gear, mosquito repellent on the waterfalls tour.

Wear and bring

Closed-toe shoes are required. Layers and a light jacket; a swimsuit and towel for the waterfalls tour.

Lava

Never promised. Conditions change hourly; the Twilight tour gives the best chance.

Cancellation

Full refund with 24 hours' notice for one to five guests, 48 hours for six or more, 72 hours for private tours. Tours run rain or shine.

Questions

Good to know

How many people are on the tour?

Thirteen at most, in a Mercedes Sprinter with one guide.

Will we see lava?

Maybe. It depends on the volcano that day. The Twilight tour reaches the crater lookouts at dusk, when any glow is visible.

What is included?

Lunch, all park and attraction admissions, jackets and rain gear. Dinner too on the Twilight tour.

What does it cost?

Circle Island $249, Twilight Volcano $279, Waterfalls $279, Kona history $179, plus tax. Children 12 and under about $10 less. Checked September 30, 2026.

Where do they pick up?

Hotels in Keauhou, Kona, Waikoloa and Kohala on the group tours; Hilo on private tours.
