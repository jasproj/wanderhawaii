The company

A 1978 name with a new owner

The operator says Ali'i Nui started in 1978; it names no founder. In March 2023 Ali'i Nui and Maui Dive Shop were bought by RED Hospitality and Leisure, which an SEC exhibit prices at $11.3 million including $2.0 million in units. Maui Now lists Anthony Valeriano as Brand Standards and Product Manager. Captains Ryan and Bree appear in recent reviews.

The fleet: Ali'i Nui, a 65-foot by 36-foot catamaran; Ali'i Kai, a 65-foot catamaran launched September 1, 2024; and two 37-foot Boston Whaler 350 Realm boats for private groups, Ali'i Nani from Kihei Boat Landing and Ali'i Nalu from Maalaea Slip 74. The operator lists USA Today 10Best Readers' Choice 2023 and runs an “Ali'i Guarantee” of rebooking or refund if the experience falls short.

The one to book

Molokini Deluxe Snorkel and Sail

The company describes limited capacity; the 2009 builder spec was 100 passengers, so ask for current numbers.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Slip 56, Maalaea Harbor, 300 Maalaea Road, Wailuku.

Included

Open bar, snorkel gear and flotation, a photographer and reef-safe sunscreen on snorkel sails; Maui Brewing beverages and naturalists on whale watches, per the operator.

Ages

Children 3 and under sail free as lap children. Royal Sunset Dinner and top-deck seats are adults only.

Cancellation

A full refund more than 48 hours ahead; inside 48 hours typically none unless trip protection was bought. The captain may cancel for weather and offer a rebook or refund.

Transport

Resort round-trip transport is extra on select trips.

Questions

Good to know

Who owns Ali'i Nui?

RED Hospitality and Leisure, since March 2023.

Where do the boats leave from?

Maalaea Harbor, Slip 56. The Kihei private yacht leaves from Kihei Boat Landing.

What does it cost?

Molokini sail $259, Turtles and Tacos $149, sunset sail $149, dinner $229, whale watch $119. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can kids come?

Yes on most sails; 3 and under free. Dinner and top deck are adults only.

Is there a Kaanapali departure?

We found none; trips leave from Maalaea and Kihei.
