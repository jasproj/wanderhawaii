The company

From itineraries for friends to a tour company

Per a 2016 profile in Edge Media, owners Tony DeLellis and Gary Marrow left Los Angeles corporate careers for Kapoho in about 2002 and began by drawing up itineraries for visiting friends and family. They then began meeting cruise passengers in Hilo. BBB lists Anthony DeLellis III as principal and Gary Marrow II as manager, with the business starting in April 2004; the site does not give a founding year.

The operator describes its Evening Volcano Explorer as run by NPS-certified guides, and BBB lists the company as accredited since 2006 with an A+ rating. It states a Best Zipline and Best Lava/Volcano Tour vote in 2019 and press features in Conde Nast Traveler, People and Hawaii Magazine, none of which we could confirm independently. In 2018 Marrow told CNBC bookings fell about 30 percent during the Kilauea eruption.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to meet

Hilo tours: the Grand Naniloa Hotel store at 93 Banyan Drive. Kona and Kohala tours use pickup points listed at booking. Phone 808-964-1000.

Bring

Closed-toe shoes for the lava hikes, a rain layer, a warm jacket for Maunakea and water.

Weather

Stargazing depends on cloud cover. One 2022 review of the Kona-pickup stellar tour said no stars were visible.

Pickup

Reviews mention long hotel pickup loops on the Kona departures; ask for your pickup time.

Cancellation

Policies are in the booking flow; we could not confirm them on the operator's pages.
