The company

Built around self-guided trips

The operator's About page names Michael Gulden as founder and owner and says he started the company to share some of the best scenery and adventures on Oahu in a more convenient, family-friendly way. The page gives the start as both 2013 and 2016, so we leave the year out. Tripadvisor has named it a Travelers' Choice winner in 2022 and 2024, per the operator.

The business leans on self-guided rentals and tours: kayaks, stand-up paddleboards, e-bikes and snorkel sets, with the gear, straps and instruction provided. It also runs guided trips to the Mokulua Islands and Popoia Island, and a Kailua e-bike food tour. Nearly every trip has a Kama'aina or military rate.

The one to book

Mokulua Islands guided kayak tour

Five hours with a guide: ocean kayaking and snorkeling to the Mokulua Islands off Kailua. The price includes bird-sanctuary permits, kayaks, beach gear and instruction. Ages 13 and up on the group trip; private tours are available for children 12 and under. Guests should be physically active.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The Kailua storefront at 134B Hamakua Drive.

Vehicle

The Kahana river trip is 40 minutes from Kailua; bring a four-door vehicle or roof racks for the kayaks, per the operator.

Ages

The Mokulua group trip is 13 and up with private tours for younger children. Several guided trips are for 16 and up with small children allowed.

Bring

Reef-safe sunscreen, water, a hat, water shoes and a dry change.

Policies

Weight limits and cancellation terms are in the booking flow; we could not confirm them on the operator's pages.

Questions

Good to know

Is it guided or self-guided?

Both. Mokulua and Popoia are guided; Chinaman's Hat, the sandbar and the river are self-guided.

What does it cost?

Mokulua guided $205, Popoia $145, self-guided kayaks $82, e-bike rental $57, tandem kayak $69. Checked September 30, 2026.

Is there a Kama'aina rate?

Yes, on most trips, for Hawaii residents and military.

Can kids come?

Yes on Popoia and the self-guided trips; the Mokulua group trip starts at 13.

Where is the shop?

134B Hamakua Drive, Kailua.
