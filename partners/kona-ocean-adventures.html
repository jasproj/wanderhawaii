The company

A boutique boat with a dive locker

Kona Ocean Adventures calls itself a small boutique company and says private charters are its specialty; the mixed-group trips run six to eight guests on boats certified for more. Owners Danny and Laure are both licensed captains; Tripadvisor lists the business as veteran-owned and women-owned, and the crew works in English, French, Japanese and Spanish. Captain Danny's homemade salsa gets its own line on the About page.

The boats carry shade, a marine head, a hot handheld shower for after the manta swim, oxygen, and LED light boards for the night trips; they are Coast Guard inspected quarterly. Instructors hold PADI, SSI and SDI cards. Tripadvisor has given the company its Travelers' Choice or Certificate of Excellence every year since 2012.

The one to book

The manta night snorkel or dive

Two and a half hours after sunset at the manta site off Kona, where more than two hundred individually identified reef mantas feed on the plankton that the lights pull in. Snorkelers hold a lit board at the surface and the mantas roll underneath; certified divers go down with a tank. Wetsuits, gear and the boards are included. Ages 6 and up in the water.

Hot shower and hot cocoa on the boat afterward. The same price covers snorkelers, certified divers and ride-alongs.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Honokohau Harbor, 74-380 Kealakehe Parkway. Enter the harbor from Highway 19, first right after a quarter mile, past the deli, then left toward the ramps; the boats are along the fence on the left. Blackwater checks in 45 minutes early.

Included

Wetsuits, snorkel and dive gear, reef-safe sunscreen, light boards on night trips, a hot shower after, and food or cocoa depending on the trip.

Bring

Towel, reef-safe sunscreen, a waterproof camera, a reusable bottle, a rash guard. No dangling jewelry on manta trips.

Minimums

Four guests to run a mixed trip. Kids 10 to 12 on Discover Scuba need a private instructor.

Cancellation

48 hours for parties up to six, 72 hours for seven or more and private charters. Weather cancellations are rebooked or refunded.

Questions

Good to know

How big are the groups?

Six to eight on mixed trips, seven divers on a two-tank, twelve on a private charter.

Can non-divers come on the manta trip?

Yes. Snorkelers hold a lit board at the surface; divers go down. Same price either way.

Do I need to be certified?

Not for snorkel trips or Discover Scuba. Two-tank dives need a card; the blackwater dive needs an advanced card, 50 dives and a dive in the last six months.

What does it cost?

Manta $250, Kealakekua $295, two-tank dive $295, Discover Scuba $425, blackwater $310, private charter from $2,300. Checked September 30, 2026.

Where is it?

Honokohau Harbor, between the airport and town, on the ramp side. Look for the tentacles.
