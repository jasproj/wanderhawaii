Fathom Five Divers

Kauai's south shore has one dive shop that says it only runs six-diver boats. Fathom Five Divers, on Poipu Road in Koloa, is a PADI 5 Star dive center owned and operated by Jeannette Val Auber. The two-tank boat dive is the everyday trip; the Niihau three-tank is the famous one, and it runs only in summer.

The company

The only six-pack dive operation on Kauai, it says

The operator's About page lists Jeannette Val Auber as owner and operator, and describes a crew of about seventeen, including Loni Adams, Bill Hammond, Brenden Brockman, Devon Turner, Grace, Drake and Sergio. The shop describes its boats as custom six-passenger boats built in Hawaii, and calls itself the only six-pack diving operation on Kauai. It lists a PADI 5 Star IDC rating, a Best Dive Shop for Beginners mention in the Ultimate Kauai Guidebook, and a 2026 Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice award.

The shop runs boat dives from Koloa, shore dives from Koloa Landing, Discover Scuba, and PADI courses from Open Water through Rescue. The summer Niihau trip is a three-tank, eleven-hour day with a two-and-a-half-hour boat ride each way, for certified divers aged 15 and up.

The one to book

Two-tank boat dive

Five hours, two dives, daily, on a boat carrying at most six divers. The booking page marks it the most popular trip. Certified divers ages 12 and up; ages 10 and 11 call to book. A non-diving rider can take a standby seat.

Dive sites are chosen by conditions on the day. Gear and nitrox are rented or added separately; check the shop.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

3450 Poipu Road, Koloa, which is where the Niihau and boat trips depart.

Certification

Boat and shore dives need a card. Discover Scuba needs no experience. Open Water, Advanced, Enriched Air and Rescue courses are on the booking page, several by call.

Gear

Gear and nitrox are available separately; confirm sizes when you book.

Weather

Sites are chosen on the day. Ask the shop about its weather and cancellation terms; none were published where we looked.

Seasons

Niihau runs May to September only. Tunnels shore dives run spring through fall.

Questions

Good to know

How many divers are on a boat?

Six at most, per the operator.

What does it cost?

Two-tank boat dive $239, premium three-tank $345, twilight two-tank $259, Koloa Landing shore dives from $145, Discover Scuba from $170. Checked September 30, 2026.

Is Niihau running now?

No. The Niihau three-tank trip runs May to September.

Can I dive without a certification?

Yes: Discover Scuba, ages 10 and up.

Can a non-diver come?

The boat trips sell a rider seat at $135 standby.
