The company

Kailua Sailboard Company, 1982

The shop opened in 1982 as Kailua Sailboard Company, moved to the Kailua Beach Center in 1985, and became Kailua Sailboards & Kayaks, Inc. in 1995. It has since been renamed Kailua Beach Adventures. The operator does not name its owners on its public pages. It describes its guides as certified lifeguards and true watermen and women.

The stewardship side is the company's second pitch: it organizes beach clean-ups with school groups and non-profits, keeps a marine-plastics recycling station at the shop, and has guides do wildlife sanctuary restoration. The operator lists Surfrider's John Kelly Award for business of the year and Sustainable Tourism certification, and Tripadvisor gave it a 2026 Travelers' Choice award.

    Owner and team names are not published; the shop has a team page, “Our Ohana.” Guides Tomo, Maggie, Mike and Brian are named in recent reviews.

    The one to book

    Twin Islands guided kayak tour

    Five hours. Paddle to the Mokulua bird sanctuaries, walk the island with your guide, snorkel the coral and the turtle feeding grounds, then beach time. Island landing permits are included, and the price covers snorkel gear, a dry bag, showers and changing rooms. Groups are capped at eight.

    Adults 13 and up ride the shared tour; ages 13 to 16 sit in a double with a parent. Children 8 and up can go on a private tour. Everyone must be able to swim.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Where to check in

    130 Kailua Road, Kailua, in the same center as Bob's Pizza and Island Snow. Arrive 15 minutes before your tour. Open 8 to 5, every day of the year. Parking is free for rental and tour customers.

    Bring

    Water, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses with a strap, water shoes, a rashguard or quick-dry clothes, a dry bag, a towel and a change of clothes.

    Limits

    Everyone must swim. Under 18 needs an adult. Under 16 paddles a double with an adult. Twin Islands takes ages 13 and up on the shared tour.

    Permits

    Island landing permits are included on guided tours and self-guided packages; a standalone permit is $3 per person per day, Monday to Saturday. None are issued on Sundays.

    Cancellation

    Full refund or free reschedule more than 48 hours out. Inside 48 hours, the full charge or $25 per person to reschedule. No weather policy is posted.

    Good to know

    How many people are on the Twin Islands tour?

    Eight at most, with a guide.

    Do I need experience?

    No, but everyone must be able to swim. The 2-hour tour suits families and beginners; ages start at 8.

    What does it cost?

    Twin Islands $249, 2-hour guided kayak $179, SUP lesson $179, self-guided days from $99. Checked September 30, 2026.

    Are there tours on Sunday?

    The operator says island landing permits are not issued on Sundays, so check the booking calendar for guided-tour dates.
