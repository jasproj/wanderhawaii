Dive Maui

A dive shop on Front Street that has kept going through the worst of Lahaina's recent years. Dive Maui is run by CJ Kessler, who learned to dive at the shop as a kid, and her partner Nichlas Holm. The boat trip to the Lanai Cathedrals is the one to book; the shore dives, the night dive and the winter whale watches fill in the rest.

The company

Learned to dive here, came back to run it

CJ Kessler learned to dive at Dive Maui as a kid and worked there on school breaks. She went on to dive in Thailand, qualified as a PADI Divemaster and Instructor in Indonesia, and met Nichlas Holm there; the two came back to Maui in August 2018 and own the shop now. The shop's line is that people protect what they love. The operator lists Scuba Diving Magazine Readers' Choice recognition from 2018, and Lanai Cathedrals as a Top 100 Gold Site that year.

After the August 2023 Lahaina fire the team posted that everyone was safe and pointed visitors to community aid. The shop's own pages say it has had to limit its tour list since, citing lost vessels and an instructor housing shortage. The boats that run are Hoku, a 37-foot rigid-hull inflatable for up to 22, and Seahawk, a 27-foot aluminum boat for six. Several boat trips and courses are marked unavailable for now.

The one to book

Lanai Cathedrals two-tank boat dive

Six hours on the boat across the channel to Lanai, with two dives among the lava arches and caverns that made the Cathedrals a Top 100 site, and snacks between. Meet at the shop at 7 am; the trip runs Monday, Friday and Saturday and gets back around 12:30 to 1. Certified divers only, ages 14 and up, with nitrox available for a fee.

The operator lists a snorkeler fare for this trip; check the booking page for it. Gear is rented item by item.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The shop at 1223 Front Street, Lahaina. Whale watches leave from Mala Ramp. Parking is the small lot in front of the shop, with overflow across the street and at Lahaina Cannery Mall.

Certification

Boat dives need a card. Discover Scuba needs no experience, but the PADI eLearning must be done first and a medical form signed. Lanai Cathedrals is ages 14 and up.

Bring

Swimsuit, towel and reef-safe sunscreen. Gear is included on Discover Scuba and rented by the item on boat dives.

Whale watch

Season runs from mid-December. The operator guarantees sightings or a free return trip; ages 3 and up.

Cancellation

48 hours on all dives, medical reasons included; 24 hours on whale watches. No weather policy is posted.

Questions

Good to know

Is Dive Maui open after the fire?

Yes. Shore dives, the Lanai Cathedrals boat dive and whale watches take bookings. Some boat trips and courses are unavailable while the shop rebuilds its schedule.

Do I need to be certified?

For boat dives, yes. Discover Scuba is for first-timers, ages 10 and up, after a short PADI eLearning and a medical form.

What does it cost?

Lanai Cathedrals $269, Discover Scuba from shore $140, night dive $140, whale watch $85 adult and $60 child. Checked September 30, 2026.

Is there a whale guarantee?

The operator guarantees sightings or a free return trip. Season dates on the shop's pages vary slightly; dates here open in mid-December.

Where do I meet?

The shop on Front Street, at 1223 Front Street. Whale watches leave from Mala Ramp.
