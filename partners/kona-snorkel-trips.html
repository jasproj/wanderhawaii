The company

One boat, small groups, no upsell

Kona Snorkel Trips describes itself as friend-owned and operated. It runs a single boat, the Orca, a 28-foot rigid-hull inflatable that was built for the U.S. Navy SEALs and now carries cushioned seats with backrests, a canopy and dry storage. Captains are Coast Guard certified; the in-water guides are lifeguard certified. The company hands out reef-safe sunscreen and keeps every trip to 19 guests.

The boat is quick and the ride can be bouncy, so the company turns away expectant mothers, anyone with back or neck trouble, and recommends against guests over 75. First-time snorkelers are welcome on the day trips, with flotation supplied; the manta snorkel is for swimmers who have snorkeled before. The company does not publish crew names, so none are listed here.

The one to book

Night Manta Ray Snorkeling Tour

Two hours from Honokohau Harbor, timed to sunset. The boat anchors at Garden Eel Cove or in front of the Keauhou hotel, the crew floats a custom snorkel board rigged with high-powered lights, and guests hold the rail while the mantas loop up to feed on the plankton the lights attract. Mask, snorkel, shorty wetsuit and flotation are included, per the tour page.

Ages 5 and up. You must be able to swim 50 yards without flotation and have snorkeled before; non-swimmers and first-timers are not allowed on this trip. The company suggests booking it early in your stay, so there is room to rebook if the ocean has other plans.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where

Honokohau Harbor, Kailua-Kona. The company lists 74-381 Kealakehe Parkway.

Requirements

Manta snorkel: swim 50 yards unaided and have snorkeled before. Day trips: basic swimming to enter the water; non-swimmers may ride along.

Not permitted

Expectant mothers and guests with back, neck or shoulder issues, on any trip. Not recommended over 75 or over 300 lb.

Cancellation

Two rates on every trip: non-refundable, or free cancellation up to 48 hours before. Private charters, 7 days.
