Hilo Ocean Adventures

Most Big Island boat operators work from the Kona and Kohala side, where the water is calm. Hilo Ocean Adventures works the wet east coast, and the trip that sets it apart is the waterfall cruise along the Hamakua cliffs. It is a women-owned dive shop first, and it has added snorkel, kayak, whale watch and swim-school programs since 2012.

The company

A dive shop that kept adding programs

According to the operator's About page, Hilo Ocean Adventures was founded in 2012 by Jenna, who grew up in a family of divers and studied business on Maui. The scuba operation came first. Snorkel, kayak and paddle tours, whale watching, fishing charters and a swim school were added over the years. Tripadvisor lists the business as women-owned.

The page says Ryan manages operations with support from his aunt and uncle. Its staff list, shown by first name and last initial, includes instructors, a front-desk manager, two boat captains and a first mate. The operator runs a heated indoor pool for swim lessons and scuba refreshers. The boat trips run on a Scarab jet boat that seats up to six.

The one to book

Onomea Waterfall Cruise

A boat ride up the Hamakua coast to see the sea cliffs and waterfalls that spill straight into the ocean, with a guide narrating and snacks aboard. The trip runs 1.5 to 2 hours; transport from the check-in shop to the boat harbor is part of the price. Ages 8 and up, and minors need an adult.

Expect spray. The operator does not recommend it for guests with spinal injuries, pregnancy or cardiovascular conditions.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The shop at 1717 Kamehameha Avenue, Hilo, open 9 am to 5 pm daily per the operator. On harbor tours you are shuttled from check-in to the boat.

Limits

Boat tours start at age 8, and minors need an adult. Whale watches ask guests to be able to climb back aboard by ladder and to weigh under 300 pounds, per a reseller listing; ask the operator for its own rule.

Bring

A rain layer and a towel; it is Hilo. The operator provides snorkel gear, showers and a changing room.

Whale season

Royal Humpback Whale Experience runs December 16 to April 30.

Cancellation

Policies vary by reseller. Direct bookings show the terms at checkout; the operator's waterfall cruise lists a full refund 24 hours or more ahead.
