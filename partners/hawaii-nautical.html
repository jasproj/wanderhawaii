The company

A three-island fleet

The operator describes itself as locally owned, in business for over twenty-five years, with the largest inter-island fleet in the state, and says it was the first operator in Hawaii recognized by NOAA's Dolphin SMART program. It publishes no owner or captain list. BBB shows Hawaii Nautical Inc., a Hawaii corporation, with Douglas Ewalt as president and a business start date of October 2001.

The fleet listed on its About page: Spirit of Aloha I, II and III; Ko Nau Lani; Honu Lani; No Wiki; Amore; Kohola; Olohana; and Holona. Ko Nau Lani is a 65-foot sailing catamaran with shade, restrooms and a freshwater shower; Spirit of Aloha in Kona is a 54-foot Gold Coast catamaran for 49; Amore on Maui is a 42-foot Boston Whaler for six. The company also sells Kaula Kai sails with a luau tie-in at Ko Olina.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Your confirmation names the harbor and slip. The Aulani-guest catamaran leaves from Waianae Harbor, 85-491 Farrington Highway; arrive 20 minutes early. Phone 808-234-7245.

Included

Lunch on the snorkel sail; drinks and appetizers on the sunset sail; snorkel gear on snorkel sails.

Onboard

Reviewers on the Oahu boats mention loud music below deck and no footwear allowed.

Cancellation

Maui and Kona pages say full refund 48 hours or more ahead; inside 48 hours the fare is forfeited unless trip protection was bought. Confirm Oahu terms when you book.

Ages

Child fares 4 to 11 and infants 0 to 3 free on Oahu; Kaula Kai sails are adults 21 and up.
