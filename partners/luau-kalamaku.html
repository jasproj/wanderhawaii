Luau Kalamaku

A luau with a script. Luau Kalamaku stages a story of a voyage from Tahiti to Hawaii, with hula, chanting, fire poi and fire knife, at Kilohana Plantation outside Lihue. Dinner comes from an imu and a buffet, the bar is open, and the room holds several hundred. Creative director and producer Haunani “Nani” Asing Marston created the show.

The company

A show built on a plantation estate

The operator's Creative page lists Haunani “Nani” Asing Marston as Creative Director and Producer and credits her as creator of the show. Its team includes a stage manager, a Tahitian-segment choreographer, two kumu hula and a cast of about twenty-five. The Kilohana estate was established in 1896 by Albert Spencer Wilcox; Gaylord Parke Wilcox built the mansion in 1936, and its restaurant, Gaylord's, hosts the Manor House package.

The venue seats up to 800, per a 2022 audio-system case study; reviewers describe 500-plus in the room. A craft fair and grounds open before dinner, and an optional add-on takes guests on the Kauai Plantation Railway through the estate. Whether the railway and the luau share an owner is not established in what we found.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Kilohana Plantation, 3-2087 Kaumualii Highway, Lihue. Customer support is at 877-622-1780.

Schedule

Evening shows on select nights; the operator's own pages and resellers disagree on which weekdays, so use the booking calendar. A typical evening starts with a craft fair and grounds around 5 pm, the imu ceremony, dinner, then the show.

Included

Buffet dinner, open bar with mai tai, live music and the artisan market. The train and hotel transportation are separate options.

Kids

Children 2 and under are free; the child fare covers 3 to 12 and the teen fare 13 to 17.

Policies

Cancellation and weather terms are on the booking flow; we could not confirm them on the operator's pages.

Reviewers praise the show, the fire acts, the covered venue, the open bar and waitstaff, the plantation setting and the train. Criticism: basic buffet food, a crowded room, a story that is hard to follow, sound trouble in one review, no poi and high prices, especially for premium seating.

What does the luau include?

Dinner, the imu ceremony, the show and an open bar with mai tais. Transportation and the plantation train are separate options.

What does it cost?

Hawaiian $181.26, Silver $200.34, Gold $222.60 adult; Manor House $262.88. As shown on FareHarbor on September 30, 2026.

Are children welcome?

Yes: free at 2 and under, $62.54 for ages 3 to 12 at the Hawaiian package.

Which night?

Dates differ by month. Use the booking calendar; we could not confirm the weekday pattern.
