The company

Seeking the mana in the water

Jacob Kahiapo is the founder and runs operations; Mash Hatae is the partner and handles IT and guest experience. The company says it was founded to seek and share the mana in Hawaii's waters, coastline and people. It is a Four Seasons Oahu preferred charter partner and was named to Hawaii Business Magazine's Best of Small Business list in 2025. The crews are licensed captains and deckhands who grew up on these shores.

Three boats: MANAKAHI, a 46-foot Leopard sailing catamaran at Ko Olina; HILINA'I, a 43-foot Leopard power cat at Ala Wai; and VENTURE, a 32-foot World Cat sport cat at Ko Olina. Every trip has a hosted bar, snorkel gear, fishing gear and floats. The boats run barefoot, and guests may bring their own food with no corkage fee; red wine is banned because it stains.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Ko Olina: Ko Olina Marina, 92-100 Waipahe Place, Kapolei, in the marina lot. Waikiki: Ala Wai Boat Harbor, 5 Holomoana Street, across from the Prince Waikiki hotel, with metered street parking on Ala Moana. Check in 15 minutes before departure.

Included

Masks, snorkels and fins for all ages, floats and belts, fishing gear, a hosted bar with non-alcoholic drinks, and lunch or a tasting menu depending on the trip. Dietary needs with notice.

Bring

Swimwear, a hat, a light jacket for the evening, a towel and reef-safe sunscreen. The boats run barefoot. Bring your own food if you like; no red wine.

Fishing

Visitors 15 and older need a Hawaii saltwater fishing license.

Cancellation

Full refund with 48 hours' notice; none inside 48 hours. The operator may cancel up to an hour before departure for conditions, with a refund or a rebooking.

Questions

Good to know

How many people are on a shared sail?

Six at most, with a captain and a mate.

What is the difference between shared and private?

Shared is a per-person fare on a scheduled sailing. Private is the whole boat for your group, on any day, at a flat price listed for six.

What does it cost?

Ko Olina snorkel and lunch $325, sunsets $225 to $240, Waikiki snorkel and lunch $270, private charters from $700 an hour. Checked September 30, 2026.

Which days do shared sails run?

Ko Olina on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday; Waikiki on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.

Can I bring my own food?

Yes, with no corkage fee. Red wine is not allowed.
