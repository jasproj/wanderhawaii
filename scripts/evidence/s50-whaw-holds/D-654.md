**D-654 — Never-anchor tests are unicode-normalised: NFC, okina variants (ʻ ‘ ’ ` ´) folded to
ASCII apostrophe, macrons/diacritics stripped, before any word test.** Jason's ruling, 2026-08-26,
WHAW s50 (PR-D, s50-whaw-holds). Why: s50-whaw-refresh (#259) anchored 20 rows on a Kamaʻāina
(Hawaii-resident) or Resident/Military tier because the live spelling "Kamaʻāina" (U+02BB okina,
ā macron) evaded an ASCII-only regex; visitors cannot book resident rates, so a resident tier is a
concession and never anchors. Applies to every FareHarbor customer-type classifier network-wide
(the s48/s49 WENG/WAMS classifiers share the ASCII form). Companion: "resident" and "local rate"
join the never-anchor vocabulary; the duration test for hire/rental floors is case-insensitive
("WEEK RENTAL" is a duration).
